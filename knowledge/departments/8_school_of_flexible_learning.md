# School of Flexible Learning

The School of Flexible Learning offers part-time courses, micro-credentials, and online programs designed to help learners upskill or reskill around work and family commitments. It sits within Centennial's Faculty of Lifelong Learning and Industry Training (LLIT), which supports adult learners, professionals in transition, and anyone seeking flexible, purpose-driven education.

## Offerings
- **Part-time Programs** — quality, flexible programs for busy learners, offered through evening, weekend, and online scheduling.
- **Part-time Courses** — over 1,300 individual part-time courses delivered across Centennial's academic Schools, letting students take single courses to enhance their learning without committing to a full program.
- **Micro-Credentials** — short, skills-focused programs (roughly weeks to months) developed with industry partners to build in-demand competencies quickly; Centennial offers 30+ micro-credentials across all of its academic Schools, and some are "stackable" toward full diplomas or certificates.
- **Part-time Online Learning** — programs and courses that can be completed entirely on the learner's own schedule.

## Certificates vs. Micro-Credentials
Certificates are longer programs (typically 8–12 months) offering broader, hands-on training for entry-level careers, often including practical experience and serving as a pathway to diplomas or further study — in contrast to the shorter, narrower-scope micro-credentials.
