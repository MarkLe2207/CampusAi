# School of Transportation

The School of Transportation is the largest on-site transportation training facility in Canada. Faculty are highly skilled industry professionals, and training equipment reflects current industry practice. The School maintains long-standing industry partnerships, including with General Motors, Ford, Toyota, Canadian Tire, Volvo, Freightliner, Honda, and Air Canada.

## Campuses
- **Ashtonbee Campus** (Scarborough) — home to automotive, heavy-duty, truck and coach, motorcycle, and power sports programs; one of the largest automotive and transportation technology training centres in Canada. Ashtonbee also hosts selected Community and Health Studies programs.
- **Downsview Campus** — home to the College's aerospace and aviation programs, including Aviation Technician – Aircraft Maintenance and related Engineering Technology programs in Aerospace Manufacturing. The campus reuses the historic de Havilland Canada aircraft factory and, at full capacity, is designed to host roughly 1,000 students across aviation maintenance engineering, engineering technology, and airframe assembly programs — a significant expansion from the previous Ashtonbee hangar space.

## Program Areas
- Automotive Service Technician (including co-op and modified apprenticeship options, e.g., with Canadian Tire)
- Auto Body Repair Techniques
- Heavy Duty (truck and coach) programs
- Truck and Trailer, and modified apprenticeship programs
- Aviation Technician – Aircraft Maintenance
- Aerospace Manufacturing (delivered jointly with the School of Engineering Technology and Applied Science)

## Scale
The School trains over 3,000 apprentices across its automotive, truck and coach, truck and trailer, and modified apprenticeship programs, and its post-secondary diploma programs graduate more than 300 students per year. Automotive labs are equipped with current technology for cars, trucks, and heavy-duty vehicles, and the School operates a fully equipped hangar for aerospace and avionics training.
