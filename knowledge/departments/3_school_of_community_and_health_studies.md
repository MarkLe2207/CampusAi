# School of Community and Health Studies

The School of Community and Health Studies (SCHS) prepares students for careers centred on caring for and supporting others, spanning both health-focused and social/community-focused fields.

## Home Campus
SCHS is based primarily at Centennial's Morningside Campus in Scarborough, Ontario, an environmentally friendly building situated in the Rouge Valley Conservation Area. Morningside also hosts selected Engineering Technology and Applied Science programs. Some Community and Health Studies programs are also delivered at the Ashtonbee Campus.

## Program Areas
The School's programs include:
- Pharmacy Technician
- Paramedic
- Nursing
- Massage Therapy
- Food and Nutrition
- Esthetician
- Occupational Therapist Assistant and Physiotherapist Assistant
- Medical Laboratory Technician
- Early Childhood Education
- Social Service Work
- Child and Youth Care
- Community Development
- Recreation and Leisure Services
- Workplace Wellness and Health Promotion (graduate certificate) — one of the first programs of its kind in Canada, focused on wellness coaching, health risk assessment, and workplace/community health promotion strategy
- A number of liberal arts and pre-health preparatory programs

## Facilities
The School's health studies labs simulate real clinical and field environments for Nursing, Paramedic, OTA/PTA, Pharmacy Technician, Massage Therapy, and Esthetician students. These facilities are also used by outside emergency-response organizations and Greater Toronto Area hospitals for large-scale simulation exercises, and the College runs an annual "Mock Disaster" event where students in health, community care, and public safety programs apply their skills in a realistic, high-pressure simulated emergency.

## Student Experience
Recreation and Leisure Services students complete over 800 hours of field placement, well above the 400–500 hours typical elsewhere, and the program has repeatedly won Parks and Recreation Ontario's annual awards for recreation and leisure education.
