# School of Hospitality, Tourism and Culinary Arts

This School prepares students for the dynamic hospitality, tourism, and culinary arts industry through a balance of business and creative skills, delivered in purpose-built facilities and culinary labs as well as through immersive online programs aligned with industry standards.

## Facilities
The School is housed in a roughly 350,000-square-foot facility at Progress Campus, which also includes a Centennial Student Residence with accommodation for about 740 students. The facility includes state-of-the-art culinary labs, baking labs, a beverage tasting room, and a restaurant, all used to give students hands-on, real-world experiential learning ahead of graduation.

## Program Areas
Programs span four broad categories — Culinary Arts, Hospitality Management, Tourism Studies, and Event Management — including:
- Baking and Pastry Arts Management (2-year, Online, and Compressed options)
- Baking Skills (1-year, and Online)
- Culinary Management (2-year, Online, and Compressed options)
- Culinary Skills (1-year, and Online)
- Food Media (Graduate Certificate)
- Food Tourism (Graduate Certificate)
- Hospitality and Tourism Administration (3-year)
- Tourism (2-year)
- Hospitality – Hotel Operations Management (2-year, Fall/Summer intake, Online, and Compressed options)
- Hospitality Skills (1-year)
- Special Event Planning

Credentials range from apprenticeships and certificates to diplomas, degrees, and graduate certificates, allowing students to choose a pathway matched to their career goals.

## Approach
Faculty bring direct industry experience, and the curriculum is designed to build both practical culinary/hospitality talent and management skills so graduates are prepared for real-world challenges in a fast-changing global market.
