# The Business School

The Business School at Centennial College delivers more than 30 programs that reflect the breadth of the business world, from accounting and marketing to human resources, international business, supply chain, and general business administration. The curriculum balances academic theory with experiential, hands-on learning and places a strong emphasis on innovation, entrepreneurship, and business acumen so graduates enter the workforce ready to lead.

Business does not follow one single path, and the School frames its role as helping students understand how ventures grow, how business operates globally, and how leadership plays out in practice — before students decide how they want to build, change, and adapt within it. This applies whether a student's ambition is a small, family-run business or a role at a large multinational corporation.

## Programs Offered
Full-time programs include:
- Business, and Business (Online)
- Business – Human Resources (Optional Co-op)
- Business – Marketing, and Business – Marketing (Online)
- Business – Supply Chain and Operations
- Business Administration – Marketing (Optional Co-op)
- Business Administration – Supply Chain and Operations Management (Optional Co-op)
- Honours Bachelor of Business Administration – International Business (four-year degree)
- Human Resources Management (Online) (Optional Co-op)
- Marketing – Digital Engagement Strategy (Optional Co-op)
- Occupational Health and Safety Management
- Project Management (Online)
- Supply Chain Management – Logistics (Optional Co-op)

The School also offers graduate (post-graduate certificate) programs, part-time and continuing-education options, and participates in a partnership with Ontario Colleges and McDonald's Restaurants of Canada offering a Business Diploma pathway for McDonald's employees.

## Facilities and Learning Approach
Programs are delivered across Centennial's five campuses, with classrooms, labs, and studios curated for each academic School. The Business School also houses the Centennial Legal Information Centre (CLIC), which extends the College's legal studies programming into the community through free legal information sessions and research support, connected to the paralegal program area.

## Co-op and Career Support
Many programs offer optional co-op education, giving students real-world, related work experience during their studies. The School's co-op office and Career Services team support students with resume building, interview preparation, and job search planning, and Centennial reports that its graduates are rated highly for employability among Greater Toronto Area colleges.

## Financing
Students in the Business School can access application scholarships, in-course scholarships and bursaries, a Scholarships and Bursaries Finder tool, and OSAP information through Centennial's Financial Aid Services.

## Leadership
The School of Business is led by an Associate Dean, along with an Associate Dean overseeing the School of Accounting, Legal and Office Administration, which sits within the same faculty group.
