# School of Engineering Technology and Applied Science (SETAS)

SETAS prepares students to use technology and science to drive innovation, combining experiential learning with leading-edge technologies and a global perspective aligned with the future of work.

## Departments
SETAS is organized into four departments:
- Applied Biological and Environmental Sciences (ABES)
- Information and Communication Engineering Technology (ICET)
- Advanced Manufacturing and Automation Technology (AMAT)
- Sustainable Design and Renewable Energy (SDRE), which includes the Department of Mathematics and Physics

## Program Areas
Programs span applied science, computer systems, software engineering, electronics, and related technology fields, including:
- Software Engineering Technology, and Software Engineering Technology – Artificial Intelligence
- Honours Bachelor of Information Technology (Computer and Communication Networks) — a four-year degree
- Medical Laboratory Technician
- Biotechnology, and Biotechnology – Advanced (Optional Co-op)
- Food Science Technology (Optional Co-op)
- Environmental Technician / Environmental Technology (Optional Co-op)
- Electronics Engineering Technician

## Campuses
SETAS programs are delivered primarily at the Progress and Morningside campuses; ABES-department programs (Medical Laboratory Technician, Biotechnology, Food Science Technology, Environmental Technology) are based at Morningside.

## Industry Partnerships
The Samsung Tech Institute, introduced in 2015 as part of the Electronics Engineering Technician program, gives eligible students a pathway into field service technician careers with Samsung appliances. Each year, SETAS hosts a Tech Fair where graduating students showcase capstone projects and industry practitioners judge the results.
