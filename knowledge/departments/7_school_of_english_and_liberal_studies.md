# School of English and Liberal Studies

Formerly known as the School of Advancement, the School of English and Liberal Studies helps students build a solid educational foundation and strengthen communication skills, offering pathway opportunities into diploma and degree programs across the College.

## Program Areas
Full- and part-time offerings include:
- English for Academic Purposes (ESL pathway programs)
- General Arts and Science
- Liberal Arts (diploma, with transfer-credit pathways to several Ontario universities)
- Adult Academic and Youth Academic upgrading programs (many funded by government at no charge to students)
- Teaching English as a Second Language (TESL) certificate, meeting TESL Canada and TESL Ontario certification requirements
- Leadership and Inclusion certificate and other Liberal Studies electives

Through guided curricula, small classes, and both online and in-class delivery, students earn credits in English, General Education, and Liberal Studies, along with college and university transfer credits.

## The English Department
The English Department delivers courses to students across most of Centennial's post-secondary programs. Curriculum is split into a laddered ESL stream and a non-ESL stream, with placement based on the College's English Placement Assessment; most programs require at least two English courses, and some require program-specific report-writing courses (e.g., for community service or automotive programs).

## Equity and Access Initiatives
The School positions itself as a progressive leader in Indigenization, equity, diversity, and inclusion initiatives, and offers the College-mandated Global Citizenship and Equity general education course (GNED 500). Its Corrections Literacy Initiatives (CLI), run in partnership with the Toronto East Detention Centre, support justice-involved learners in building the essential skills needed for post-secondary participation.
