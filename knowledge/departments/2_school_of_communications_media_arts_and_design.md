# School of Communications, Media, Arts and Design

This School welcomes students interested in communications, media, art, design, advertising, public relations, journalism, integrated media, culture, entertainment, and performing arts. It combines creativity with business insight, preparing graduates for careers as modern-day storytellers through practical, industry-recognized programming delivered in realistic production settings.

## Home Campus and Relocation
Programs are based at the Story Arts Centre, which opened in 1994 (originally as the Centre for Creative Communications) and has built an international reputation for excellence in communications, media, arts, and design education. The Story Arts Centre is home to roughly 1,500 students and sits close to downtown Toronto near Pape Subway Station. The College has announced plans to relocate Story Arts Centre programs to Progress Campus, with a target move around Summer/Fall 2026; music, dance, theatre, and performing arts programs continue to be taught at the Performing Arts Centre.

## Program Areas
Curriculum is organized around four broad themes: Art + Design, Advertising + Public Relations, Integrated Media, and Journalism + Publishing and Culture + Entertainment. Examples of programs include:
- Advertising – Creative and Digital Strategy
- Animation – 3D
- Art and Design Fundamentals
- Digital Video Effects
- Fine Arts Studio
- Game – Art and Game – Development
- Graphic Design
- Photography
- Performing Arts Fundamentals
- Communications and Media Fundamentals (pathway certificate)

The School also offers joint programs with the University of Toronto Scarborough in Journalism, New Media Studies, and Music Industry & Technology, along with post-graduate (graduate certificate) programs in communications, advertising, entertainment, and related career-oriented fields.

## Experiential Learning
Through the STORYWORKS initiative, students complete real industry projects: professional clients work with student teams over a guided, roughly 14-week period to produce high-quality creative work, giving students direct industry experience before graduation.

## Leadership
The School is led by a Dean, who has described the School's focus on interactive storytelling across its program areas and highlighted that graduates go on to leadership roles across Canada's communications, media, and design sector.
